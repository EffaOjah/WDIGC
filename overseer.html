Meet Our General Overseer

Apostle Omotosho Tope Joseph

Lead Pastor, Word Diet International Gospel Church

Apostle Omotosho Tope Joseph is the Lead Pastor of Word Diet International Gospel Church in Calabar, Cross River State, Nigeria. His ministry carries a clear focus on evangelism, teaching, and consistent outreach beyond the church walls into everyday spaces where people live and work.

The message is simple, direct, and centered on Jesus Christ.

— Core Message
Gospel Approach

Practical approach to the Gospel.

He is known for a practical approach to the Gospel. The message is simple, direct, and centered on Jesus Christ, with a strong emphasis on salvation, spiritual growth, and real-life transformation.

Ministry Calling

Evangelism and discipleship.

His calling is deeply rooted in evangelism and discipleship. Through regular outreach programs in marketplaces and communities, he engages people directly with the message of Christ. Over time, this approach has shaped a ministry marked by visible impact and steady growth.

At Word Diet International Gospel Church, he leads a structure built around prayer, teaching, worship, and active soul winning. The focus is not only on gathering believers but also on equipping them to live out their faith with clarity and conviction.

Prayer Building a powerful, unyielding individual and corporate altar to establish God's kingdom on earth.
Teaching Preaching the pure, uncompromising, and direct spoken Word of the Lord to build deep faith.
Worship Creating an atmosphere of praise, spiritual fire, and holy consecration for divine encounters.
Soul Winning Active marketplace evangelism to depopulate the dark kingdom and raise generational leaders.
Academic Life

Lecturer of Criminology & Security Studies.

Outside ministry, Apostle Omotosho Tope Joseph serves as a lecturer at the University of Calabar, where he teaches Criminology and Security Studies. This academic role strengthens his leadership influence, especially among young adults and professionals, blending education with mentorship and values-based guidance.

University Lectureship

Teaching Criminology and Security Studies, blending intellectual rigor with moral mentorship at the University of Calabar.

Youth Mentorship Mandate

Guiding students, professionals, and future leaders into clear spiritual and professional alignment.

Family Life

Pillars of Support & Partnership

He is married to Pastor Mrs. Oluwatosin Oluwaseun Elizabeth Omotosho, and they are blessed with children. His family remains a central part of his life and ministry journey, reflecting stability, support, and shared purpose in service to God.

Global and Digital Reach

Digital & Prophetic Global Reach

His ministry extends beyond physical gatherings through online platforms, reaching audiences across different regions. Sermons, teachings, and ministry updates are shared through social media channels and YouTube, allowing a wider global audience to stay connected.

YouTube Feed Facebook Connect
Vision

Raising a standard, transforming lives.

Apostle Omotosho’s vision is to see lives transformed through the Word of God, believers strengthened in faith, and communities impacted through consistent evangelism. His work continues to reflect a commitment to raising people who live out the Gospel in practical and powerful ways.

Word Diet International Gospel Church remains a growing expression of this vision, built on the message of Christ, outreach to people, and the development of strong believers in faith.
